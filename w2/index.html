Exterior and interior Car Cosmetic Modifications

To talk about car cosmetic modifications, including both exterior and interior it has always been interesting to me how different things are able to make cars look cool. I have always enjoyed looking at cars and how nice they look but what interests me the most is being able to make a car look cool myself. This project will involve the basics on what a car might need depending on what car it is and how it looks. Not every car needs crazy modifications to look cool. Sometimes the most simple modifications is what makes the car look nice as well. I have recently started my own project car trying to make it look cool. While first starting off is well getting the money for it. After that I took ideas from the internet to get some inspiration. I will be changing the headlights, backlights, bumper, color matching the bumper and installing a carplay inside. On the interior besides the carplay I would like to install ambient lighting and some starlights on the headliner. With the website I will be creating it will help people find quicker inspirations and if they look more into something that catches their eyes I will have everything provided from what fits what car, how much the parts may cost, where you can get the parts and the final result after the parts are installed.
